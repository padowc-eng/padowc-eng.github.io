ATLAS Passive Knee Exoskeleton

Passive knee-assist exoskeleton developed through senior design to reduce knee strain during hiking without motors or batteries.

Overview

ATLAS is a passive knee exoskeleton designed to assist knee extension during walking and hiking. The system stores and returns mechanical energy without using motors or batteries.
